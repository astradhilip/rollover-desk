# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Investment-sales and capital-markets analysts, and asset managers. An analyst has just received a seller's or property manager's rent roll in Excel and must clean it before anyone can say what income rolls when. A viewer role reads results without changing them.

## Product Purpose

Messy rent roll in, explainable rollover and critical-date risk out, live-formula Excel workbook back. Success is a rent roll trusted without retyping: every doubt shown, every number traceable.

## Positioning

It refuses to guess. Missing rent, area or dates stay "not recorded", uncertain data flags or blocks confirmation, and every risk score opens up into its parts.

## Operating Context

Desk work on a laptop or large monitor, in Excel and email alongside, under deal deadlines. Occasional checks on a phone. Output goes back to Excel, so the app never tries to replace it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Upload and map a rent roll, confirm or revert it, see rollover by year, WALT, concentration, critical dates, an attention queue and a renewal and backfill pipeline. Sign-in with two roles, analyst and viewer, and data separated by organization.

Terms: rent roll, suite, WALT, rollover, notice date, mark-to-market, backfill. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`; money is dollars from integer cents.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Rollover Desk, set in plain text with no logo. The visual direction, Blueprint in light and dark, was chosen by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

All data is synthetic, with invented names. No customers, testimonials or measured time savings exist, and none may be implied.

## Product Principles

- Show the doubt: a gap on screen beats a confident wrong number.
- Every figure can explain itself.
- Keep the analyst in control: nothing goes live until confirmed, and everything can be undone.
- Excel is home: export is a first-class result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes, 44px touch targets on small screens, state never carried by colour alone.
